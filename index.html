Welcome to SaltyTree Art Therapy 歡迎來到鹽樹藝術治療

SaltyTree

Finding Stability in Any Weather 在任何天氣中，尋得一份安定

"SaltyTree" is a tree that knows how to stand firmly in any climate. This space is inspired by those trees that strive to survive amidst storms by anchoring their roots deep into the soil and breathing deeply, with resilience, through the changing of each season. 「鹽樹」是一棵懂得在任何氣候中，依然穩穩佇立的樹。這個空間的靈感源自那些在風暴中仍奮力生存的樹木，它們將根深深扎進土壤，在每個季節的變幻中，堅定地呼吸。

The storms we face are often unpredictable and relentless. Sometimes, out of a natural instinct for self-protection, we may choose to close off or need a bit of space to temporarily distance ourselves from the outside world. We honor these moments of longing for stillness; they are not signs of weakness, but your mind and body utilizing their deepest wisdom to gently protect you. 我們所面對的風暴，往往不可預測且連綿不斷。有時候，出於自我保護的本能，我們會選擇封閉或需要一點空間，暫時與外界保持距離。我們敬重這些渴望沉靜的時刻；那並非軟弱，而是你的身心正運用深層的智慧，溫柔地守護著你。

Here, there is no so-called "right" way to express yourself. We are partners co-creating this creative space with you, and you are the most profound expert on your own life. We view your expertise as the compass guiding this journey, allowing you to unfold naturally at your own pace. 在這裡，沒有所謂「正確」的表達方式。我們是與你共創創意空間的夥伴，而你，才是自身生命中最深刻的專家。我們將這份專業視為引導旅程的指南針，讓你依循自己的步調，自然綻放。

The SaltyTree Story 鹽樹的故事

Transforming Salt-Laden Ground 轉化鹹灼土地的生命力

At SaltyTree, we explore, discover, and reconnect with that sense of wonder, creativity, and resilience together. The name SaltyTree speaks of a tree that finds its way within a specific landscape, transforming a harsh, salt-laden ground into a unique expression of life. 在鹽樹，我們一同探索、發掘並重新連結那份驚奇感、創造力與韌性。鹽樹的名字，訴說著一棵在特定地景中尋得出口的樹，它將嚴酷且充滿鹽分的土地，轉化為獨一無二的生命展現。

Yet, we also deeply know that no tree can grow isolated from the ecosystem; our growth is always shaped by the environment and soil we inhabit. But please remember, you are never the burdens you carry. In art therapy, we do not view ourselves or each other as the weight itself. 然而，我們也深知沒有一棵樹能孤立於生態之外；我們的成長總受到所處環境與土壤的形塑。但請記得，你永遠不等於你所背負的重擔。在藝術治療中，我們不將自己或彼此視為重量本身。

When external challenges bring worry, anger, or fear—whether born from personal hardships or the compression of systemic injustice and the outside world—these emotions are merely clouds and rain passing through the climate of the soul. We are dedicated to walking alongside you, helping you find a place to anchor yourself through creative expression, however small or temporary that space may be. 當外部挑戰帶來憂慮、憤怒或恐懼時—無論這份沉重源於個人的生命課題，或是外界不公與體制的擠壓—這些情緒都只是心靈氣候中飄過的雲雨。我們致力於陪伴你在當下的天氣中，透過創造性表達，找到一個能安身立命的位置，哪怕那個空間再微小、再短暫。

Creativity and Arts 創造力與藝術

Experiencing Art Therapy 親歷藝術治療

Art therapy blends visual art, creative writing, and poetry to help individuals process complex emotions. The focus rests entirely on the creative process and symbolic meaning rather than artistic skill. This multi-sensory approach allows individuals to creatively express life challenges, manage anxiety, and foster self-awareness in a format distinct from traditional talk therapy. 藝術治療融合視覺藝術、創意寫作與詩歌，協助個人梳理複雜的情感。焦點完全落在創作過程與象徵意義，而非藝術技巧。這種多感官的方法讓人們能以相異於傳統談話治療的形式，創造性地表達生命挑戰、管理焦慮並培養自我覺察。

Even when environments feel restrictive or spaces are shared, art therapy provides a quiet sanctuary to reflect and reconnect with peace. Open to all skill levels and with all creative backgrounds welcome, we meet you exactly as you are as we explore creative expression anchored in grace and faith. 即便環境感到受限或空間共享，藝術治療依舊能提供一片寧靜的避風港，供人反思並重新找回平靜。我們歡迎所有技巧程度與創作背景的人，以最真實的姿態與您相遇，在恩典與信仰中探索創造性表達。

The Creative Journey 創作旅程探索

We offer diverse and adaptable channels of expression, walking with you as you externalize and reshape your inner landscape. We respect the reality of your circumstances, ensuring that you can create with privacy and a sense of safety under any environment, allowing your feeling and expression to receive the fullest respect and validation. 我們提供多元且彈性的表達管道，陪伴你將內在風景具象化並重新形塑。我們尊重你所處的現實，確保你在任何環境下，都能在隱私與安全感中創作，讓你的感受與表達得到最充分的尊重與肯定。

Through color, line, shape, and texture, we give visible, tangible form to those complex feelings that are hard to put into words. Together, we bring your inner weather, as well as your connection to the outside world, into a dialogue with the art materials. Whether the final creation is built up, layered, dismantled, or simply serves as a pure sensory release, you can allow the creative process to carry that weight. 透過色彩、線條、形狀與質地，我們將那些難以言喻的複雜感受賦予可見且具體的形狀。我們會一同將你內在的天氣，以及你與外在世界的連結，帶入媒材的對話中。無論創作最終是被構築、堆疊、拆解，或是僅僅是單純的感官抒發，你都能透過這個歷程，讓這份創造去承載那些重量。

This journey centers on your inner strengths, deeply anchored in honoring and accepting your unique pace of expression. We are your partners, willing to support you through those heavy and reflective moments, assisting you to reconnect with your inherent power, and helping you find the state you need most in your current season—whether that is a space to regain tranquility, a permission to fully rest, or a newfound sense of vitality, voice, and internal power. 這趟旅程以你的內在優勢為核心，深深錨定於敬重並接納你個人的表達步調。我們是你的夥伴，願意與你共度那些沉重與沉思的時刻，協助你重新連結與生俱來的力量，找回你在當前季節最需要的狀態—無論是重獲平靜的空間、全然休息的允許，或是重拾活力、發聲與內在力量的契機。

Art Therapist 藝術治療師

Hello, and welcome. I am Beverly Chan. 你好，歡迎。我是陳欣慈 (Beverly)。

I am an art therapist, a Christian artist, and a graduate of the Vancouver Art Therapy Institute. Throughout my journey, I have supported individuals from diverse backgrounds—including those navigating learning differences, health challenges, cognitive changes, and personal loss. Over time, I have come to see how each person’s story holds the power to grow, to connect, and to influence others. 我是一位藝術治療師、基督徒藝術家，畢業於溫哥華藝術治療學校（Vancouver Art Therapy Institute）。在我的執業歷程中，我曾支援來自不同背景的人士—包括面對學習差異、健康挑戰、認知變化以及經歷個人喪失的個案。隨著時間的推移，我深切看見每個人的故事都擁有成長、連結及影響他人的力量。

In my sessions, I aim to offer a welcoming space that facilitates artmaking through a person-centered, strengths-based approach. I believe art therapy can be a meaningful gift for many, as small acts of artmaking open space to communicate, reflect, and invite resilience. 在我的治療會談中，我提供一個包容溫暖的空間，以人為本和優勢導向的哲學來促進藝術創作。我深信藝術治療是送給許多人的珍貴禮物，因為微小的藝術創作行為能為溝通、反思與召喚韌性騰出空間。

Data Collection & Privacy Policy 資料收集與隱私政策

SaltyTree collects personal information, health intake data, and digital artwork solely to provide professional art therapy and wellness services.

Telehealth sessions are conducted using encrypted video conferencing software (such as Zoom). To ensure privacy, therapeutic sessions are never recorded, and session data is not stored within the video platform.

In compliance with international data privacy frameworks—including the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO), the Australian Privacy Act, the New Zealand Privacy Act, the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), and the Canadian Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)—all sensitive health data and client-created artwork is processed and stored using secure, encrypted systems.

We do not store therapeutic records or intake assessments on public web servers or standard email servers. You retain the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time, subject to statutory healthcare record retention laws in your local jurisdiction.
